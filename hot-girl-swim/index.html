Client
Walmart

Challenge
Walmart’s perception gap

Drive reappraisal, moving Walmart from somewhere the audience needed to shop toward somewhere they wanted to shop

Define how the hero film would communicate the opportunity through the audience mindset, insight and creative direction
Scope
Hero film, Brooklyn skate party, Miami Swim Week runway and creator wave

Somewhere You needWANT to Shop.

Walmart’s association with everyday necessities limited its appeal as a destination for fashion and trends. To become a preferred way to shop, the brand needed to earn style consideration.

The audience saw Walmart as a necessity retailer, giving it limited consideration as a destination for fashion and trends. Hot Girl Swim presented an opportunity to drive reappraisal by connecting Walmart to a cultural figure and collection the audience wanted.

Strategic Opportunity

Use Hot Girl Swim to give that audience a credible reason to reconsider Walmart’s style offering.

Every Body Is a Hot Girl Body.

The pressure to have a “summer body” can make confidence feel conditional.

Megan’s Hottie mentality offers another belief: every body can be a Hot Girl body through self-love, confidence and encouragement.

Made for Every BODY.

  • Pearl 01

    The Strategy

    Build desire for Hot Girl Swim by positioning the collection as an expression of Megan’s Hottie mentality, making the Hotties feel like this was made for every BODY.

  • Pearl 02

    The Brand Role

    Make Walmart integral to that invitation as the exclusive destination for the collection, giving the audience a reason to seek out the brand for style and reconsider its role beyond everyday necessities.

  • Pearl 03

    The Rationale

    Introduce the collection through Megan’s Hottie mentality of confidence and self-love, then connect that emotional appeal to its exclusivity at Walmart, giving the Hotties a reason to reconsider the retailer as a destination for fashion.

Treasure: The Collection

Exclusively at Walmart.

Megan Thee Stallion’s Hot Girl Swim collection, available exclusively at Walmart, presented as swimwear that feels like joy, power and play.

Confidence Isn’t Made, It’s Embodied.

Bring Megan’s Hottie mentality to life through how she shows up, moves and wears the collection.

Present Hot Girl Swim as an expression of self-love and confidence, inviting the Hotties to see every BODY as a Hot Girl body.

An Aqua-Goddess Debut.

The creative team brought “Confidence isn’t made, it’s embodied” to life through a surreal underwater mini music video starring Megan Thee Stallion as an aqua-goddess, debuting her Hot Girl Swim collection to the soundtrack of her own confidence anthem, “Her.”

An all-Black synchronized swim team and diverse Hotties-turned-aqua-goddesses populated this dreamy world, inviting fans to embrace their confidence in and out of the water.

Walmart’s Fashion Debut.

The launch extended into a Miami Swim Week runway moment, Walmart’s fashion debut, proving swimwear can feel like joy, power and play.

Into the Culture.

Beyond the film and the runway, the launch extended into a Brooklyn skate party and a paid and organic creator wave across TikTok, Instagram and YouTube, making Walmart part of the cultural conversation.

Meg Thee Siren.

Using the playbook of today’s best DTC brands to boost this partnership, we integrated Meg and her iconic taste and attitude to lure in her fellow Hotties.
